# Single-Stock Concentration Policy (v3)

## 1. Purpose
A large position in one company exposes a client to company-specific risk that diversification would otherwise remove. This policy sets limits and the review steps advisors must follow.

## 2. Limits
- A single equity position should not exceed **10 percent** of a client's total investable assets.
- Positions between 10 and 15 percent require a documented conversation with the client at the next review.
- Positions above **15 percent** must be escalated to the advisor's branch manager within 30 days, with a written diversification plan or a signed client acknowledgment of the risk.
- Employer stock held through a 401(k) or equity compensation counts toward the limit.

## 3. Reducing a concentrated position
Advisors should consider, in order of preference: gradual sales over several quarters to spread capital gains, gifting appreciated shares to charity or family, exchange funds for qualified clients, and hedging with collars only with options approval.

## 4. Exceptions
Exceptions require Investment Committee approval and must be reviewed every 12 months.
