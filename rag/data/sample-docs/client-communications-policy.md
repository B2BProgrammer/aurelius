# Client Communications Policy

## Fair and balanced
All communications with clients must be fair and balanced. Never promise or imply guaranteed returns, describe an investment as risk-free, or predict market movements with certainty.

## Required disclosures
Any client communication that mentions performance, projections or returns must include: "Investments involve risk, including possible loss of principal. Past performance and projections do not guarantee future results."

## AI-assisted drafts
Drafts produced by AI tools must be reviewed and approved by the advisor before they are sent. The advisor remains responsible for the content.

## Retention
Approved client emails are retained by the firm's archiving system for at least six years.
