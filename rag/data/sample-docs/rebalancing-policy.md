# Portfolio Rebalancing Guidelines

## When to rebalance
Review allocations at least once a quarter. Rebalance when any major asset class (equity, fixed income, cash) drifts **more than 5 percentage points** from the client's target allocation. Smaller drifts can wait for the next scheduled review.

## How to rebalance
- Prefer using new contributions, dividends and required withdrawals to move back toward target before selling.
- In taxable accounts, sell lots with losses or the highest cost basis first to limit realized gains.
- Do rebalancing trades in tax-advantaged accounts (IRA, 401(k)) first when the household has both, because trades there do not create taxable gains.

## Documentation
Record the drift, the trades proposed and the client's approval in the CRM before placing trades.
