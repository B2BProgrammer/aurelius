# Aurelius International Bond Fund (fictional) - Fact Sheet

## Objective
Income and diversification through investment-grade government and corporate bonds issued outside the United States, with currency exposure hedged to the US dollar.

## Key facts
- Expense ratio: 0.45 percent
- Average duration: 6.8 years
- Average credit quality: A
- Distribution frequency: monthly

## Risks
Interest-rate risk, credit risk and foreign political risk. Currency hedging reduces but does not remove currency effects.

## Use in portfolios
Suitable as part of the fixed-income allocation for moderate and conservative clients. Funds with similar objectives from other providers can serve as replacements when tax-loss harvesting.
