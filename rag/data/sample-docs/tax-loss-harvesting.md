# Tax-Loss Harvesting Guide

## What it is
Tax-loss harvesting means selling an investment that is below its purchase price to realize a capital loss. The loss can offset realized capital gains and, in the US, up to $3,000 of ordinary income per year, with unused losses carried forward.

## The wash-sale rule
A loss is disallowed if the client buys a "substantially identical" security within **30 days before or after** the sale. This applies across the household's accounts, including IRAs. To stay invested, advisors typically buy a similar but not identical fund, for example a different index provider for the same asset class.

## When it makes sense
Only in taxable accounts. Prioritize positions with large unrealized losses, and check that the replacement fund keeps the client's target allocation.

## Checklist
1. Confirm the account is taxable.
2. Check for purchases of the same security in the last 30 days in any household account.
3. Pick a replacement that is not substantially identical.
4. Record the trade rationale in the CRM.

Note: tax rules change. Verify current IRS guidance and consult the client's tax professional.
