# Equity Research Note: XYZ Corp (fictional large-cap technology company)

## Rating: Hold (downgraded from Buy)

## Summary
XYZ Corp reported third-quarter revenue below consensus estimates as enterprise customers delayed software renewals. Management lowered full-year guidance. The shares fell about 9 percent during the week of the report.

## Our view
The long-term business remains sound, with strong cash flow and a healthy balance sheet, but near-term growth is slowing. We see limited upside over the next 12 months and moved the rating to Hold.

## For advisors
Clients with XYZ above the 10 percent single-stock limit should review their position under the Single-Stock Concentration Policy. Consider gradual trimming rather than a single large sale.
