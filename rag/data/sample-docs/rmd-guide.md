# Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs) - Advisor Summary

## Who must take RMDs
Owners of traditional IRAs and most employer plans must start taking required minimum distributions once they reach their RMD age. Under the SECURE 2.0 Act the RMD age is **73**, rising to **75 in 2033**. Roth IRAs have no RMDs for the original owner.

## Timing
The first RMD may be delayed until April 1 of the year after the client reaches RMD age, but then two distributions are due in that year. Later RMDs are due by December 31 each year.

## Amount
The RMD equals the prior December 31 account balance divided by the life-expectancy factor from the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table (or the Joint Life table when the spouse is the sole beneficiary and more than 10 years younger).

## Missed RMDs
The excise tax on a missed RMD is 25 percent of the shortfall, reduced to 10 percent if corrected in a timely way.

## Advisor actions
Send each client an RMD estimate in January, confirm the withdrawal plan, and consider qualified charitable distributions for charitably inclined clients.
